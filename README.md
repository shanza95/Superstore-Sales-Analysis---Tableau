## Project Overview
This project uses Tableau to explore and visualize sales performance from the popular Superstore dataset. The goal was to identify trends, spot problem areas, and create an interactive dashboard that gives a clear overview of how the business is doing.

The dashboard provides a clear breakdown of sales and profit across different regions, categories, and sub-categories. It highlights top-performing products as well as areas with low profitability, helping identify opportunities for improvement. Interactive filters allow users to explore data by customer segment, date range, and product type. The use of bar charts, line graphs, and heatmaps makes trends easy to spot and understand, offering quick, actionable business insights.

## Visualization

**1. Sales Trend Over Time by Category**

-    **Furniture**

Furniture starts at about £31k in January, drops in February, and then reaches its highest point in October (£58k). The category shows several mid-year peaks in March and June, indicating occasional surges in large-ticket purchases. Sales then rise sharply to over £100k in September and finish the year very strong, reaching more than £121k in both November and December.

-    **Office Supplies**

Office Supplies follows a similar pattern, with a drop in February and a strong rise in March (£56k). The category stays stable through the middle of the year and sees another high point in October (£55k). Sales increase even more towards the end of the year, reaching around £100k from September to December.

-    **Technology**

Technology shows the most consistent upward trend. After falling to £23k in February, sales rise steadily through April, June, and July, reaching a mid-year peak of £54k in July. Sales grow even more sharply toward the end of the year, climbing from £99k in September to a peak of £131k in November, before ending the year at £100k in December.

Takeaways:
- February is the weakest month for all categories.
- Sales grow steadily in the middle of the year.
- September to December are the best months, with all three categories showing very strong sales.
- Technology has the highest peak (November).
- Furniture and Office Supplies also end the year very strong, with big sales in November and December.

**3. Profit by Category & Sub-Category**
The profit analysis across sub-categories shows a mix of strong performers and a few loss-making areas.

**Furniture**
- Chairs (£26.6k) and Furnishings (£13k) are profitable and contribute positively.
- Bookcases (-£3.5k) show a small loss.
- Tables (-£17.7k) is the weakest sub-category, generating the largest loss within Furniture.

**Office Supplies**

- Several sub-categories perform well, including Paper (£34k), Binders (£30k), and Storage (£21k).
- Envelopes (£6.9k), Art (£6k), Labels (£5.5k), and Fasteners (£950) also contribute positively.
- Supplies (-£1.2k) is the only sub-category in this group generating a loss.

**Technology**

- Technology is the strongest overall performer.
- Copiers (£55.6k), Phones (£44.5k), and Accessories (£41.9k) deliver the highest profits across all categories.
- Machines (£3.4k) is profitable but much lower compared to the other technology sub-categories.

Takeaways:

- Technology leads profit generation, especially through Copiers, Phones, and Accessories.
- Office Supplies performs well overall, with strong profits from Paper and Binders.
- Furniture is mixed, with Chairs performing well but major losses from Tables.
- The main areas dragging profit down are Tables and Supplies.


**4. Top 10 Products by Sales**
The bar chart highlights that technology and high-end office equipment dominate the top-selling products, showing strong demand for specialised, higher-priced items within the Superstore product range.

The best-selling product is the **Canon ImageCLASS 2200 Advanced**, generating £61k in sales, making it the clear leader in the dataset.

The second highest is the Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch Plastic Comb Binding Machine, with sales of £27,453, followed by the Cisco TelePresence System, which brought in around £22k. The HON 5400 Series also performed well with £21,871 in sales.

Other top-performing items include the GBC DocuBind TL300, which achieved £19,823, along with several other office and technology products that continue the trend of high-value equipment driving the majority of total sales.
